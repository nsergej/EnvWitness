# Governance

EnvWitness uses a maintainer-led, contribution-driven model.

## Roles

**Contributors** submit code, documentation, designs, reports, or community support.

**Reviewers** have demonstrated sustained technical judgment and may approve changes in their areas.

**Maintainers** own releases, security response, compatibility, governance, and repository administration.

## Decisions

Routine changes use pull-request consensus. Compatibility, security boundary, archive schema, plugin protocol, and governance changes require approval from two maintainers when the project has at least two active maintainers. Until then, the founding maintainer records the rationale publicly.

Material architecture decisions are documented in `docs/decisions.md`. Decisions may be revisited when new evidence appears.

## Becoming a Reviewer or Maintainer

Candidates are evaluated on consistent contributions, respectful reviews, security awareness, reliability, and ability to explain tradeoffs. Maintainer status is granted through a public governance pull request.

## Inactivity

A maintainer inactive for six months may move to emeritus status after a public check-in period. Access can be restored when participation resumes.
